Frequently asked questions

Some quick answers about our products and how to contact us.

Are products available to buy right now?

Products are currently marked out of stock. Please contact us to check the latest availability before travelling or placing an order.

How can I order honey?

At present, please contact Bloom & Honey by telephone or Instagram to ask about availability and arrange an order. No online checkout or payment is currently offered on this website.

Do you deliver in the UK?

Please contact us with your postcode and the products you are interested in. We will confirm whether delivery or collection can be arranged before you place an order.

Do you send orders to Europe?

UK and European delivery enquiries are welcome. Please contact us with your postcode or destination country so we can confirm whether delivery is possible, the cost and the estimated timing before you place an order.

Clear information

Customer information & policies

These notes explain how enquiries are handled. They do not replace any consumer rights you have under UK law.

Privacy policy

If you contact us by telephone or Instagram, we use the details you choose to share only to respond to your enquiry, discuss availability and arrange any agreed order. This website does not currently include an account, checkout or contact form.

Please do not send payment card details or other sensitive information through social media messages. You may ask us what personal information we hold about your enquiry.

Orders & payment

This website is an information and enquiry site. It does not accept online orders or payments. An order is not confirmed until we have discussed the product, quantity, price and collection or delivery arrangements with you.

We will confirm the total cost and any delivery charge before you agree to proceed.

Delivery & collection

Collection or delivery must be agreed with us in advance. Any delivery area, delivery fee and estimated timing will be confirmed individually before an order is accepted. UK and European delivery are not guaranteed services at this time.

Cancellations & returns

If you have agreed an order, contact us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel it. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected by this note. The return or refund position can depend on the product and how the contract was made; we will explain the applicable terms before confirming a sale.

Product information

Product descriptions and photographs are provided as a guide. Please ask us to confirm the exact product, pack size, ingredients or other relevant details before purchase. Any required food labelling information will be provided on the product packaging.

Availability

All four items are currently displayed as out of stock. Availability can change, so please contact us for an up-to-date answer. This website does not promise that a product is available for immediate purchase.
